    MyLibrary Help — Search, Shelves, Chrome Extension, Status, CLI, API & MCP

    1. Web Catalog, Search Keywords, Virtual Shelves, Cover Intelligence & On-Demand Enrichment • Structured Search Keywords & Operators: Combine free text with author:, title:, cat:, tag:, pub:, isbn:, format:epub, year:2024..2026, is:enriched, is:unenriched, is:interesting, is:badmeta, missing:cover, -exclude, and sort:newest (click Keywords Guide under the search bar for all operators).
    • Virtual Bookshelves: Browse Interesting Books, Bad Metadata, Bookmarked & Reading (with 100% Read, Unread <100%, Reading 1–99% pills), Highlights & Notes (.an), Enriched Books, Missing / Text Cover, Enriched Duplicates, Needs Enriching, In Review, Removed Protection (nodrm), Packt Books (Computer Mags & FAQ/978 sorted by most recent file modification date first), Moved to toMags, Recently Published (1 Yr), and Amazon / Kindle+ / Audible shelves.
    • Interactive Card & Modal Actions: Click ✨ Enrich Now (or ✨ Enrich Page to batch-enrich the current page), ✨ AI Generate Description from Content (reads the book's actual front matter, TOC & opening pages via Gemini AI to write & save a 2–4 sentence synopsis), ☆ Interesting (bookmarks books to read/download next; auto-clears on download), ⚑ Bad Metadata, 📖 Peek Inside (inspect front-matter pages, detected ISBNs & run AI Re-Evaluate from Image(s)), 🖼 Find Cover (searches Web Images, Goodreads, Apple Books, Google Books & Open Library; prefers full-color covers over grayscale scans, generates custom AI covers via ✨ Generate Cover, and auto-heals partial top-banner or textless background PDF covers + excessive white margins on the fly), or 📰 toMag. Read-only Tags are displayed at the bottom of both the Book Details and Review modals.
    • Enriched Duplicates Manager (/mylibrary/duplicates/): Detects duplicate copies of the same format and verified content, automatically scoring the best copy to keep while protecting unfinished Moon+ Reader bookmarks.
    • OPDS 1.2 & Calibre Content Server (/mylibrary/opds/?v=2): Add http://192.168.1.43:8080/mylibrary/opds/?v=2 in Moon+ Reader (includes /mylibrary/opds/packt/ for Packt Books). Downloading any unenriched book automatically enriches it on the fly and migrates Moon+ WebDAV bookmarks (.po / .an).
    2. Chrome Extension — Book Sites, Amazon Wishlists & Anna's Archive Checker (chrome_wishlist_extension/) • Install in Chrome: Open chrome://extensions/ → enable Developer mode → click Load unpacked → select U:\MyLibrary\chrome_wishlist_extension.
    • Amazon Wishlists (Books (Kindle), Print Books): Automatically checks every wishlist book against MyLibrary (with automatic /dp/<ASIN> publication year, edition & ISBN verification to prevent same-title false positives, plus a persistent ✕ False Positive button), highlights reduced-price items with a glowing 🏷️ ON SALE banner & 🏷️ On Sale toolbar filter chip, checks Anna's Archive by ISBN + Title, and supports Auto-Scroll & Export CSV.
    • Book Product Pages (Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Simon & Schuster, Springer Link, Goodreads, O'Reilly, No Starch + Custom Sites): Adds an inline ✅ In MyLibrary / ❌ Not in MyLibrary indicator next to the book title plus a unified panel below the cover with Open in Anna's Archive (ISBN), Search in Anna's Archive (Title), 🔍 Check Anna's Live, and optional Open in Z-Library. Switch Anna's Archive mirrors (.pk, .gl, .li, .se, .org) or add new book sites with 1 click in the extension popup.
